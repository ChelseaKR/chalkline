California educator credential authorizations, modeled onto CTDL

Unofficial demonstration. Chalkline is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing or by Credential Engine. It models information the Commission publishes on its public Authorization Sort Table, to show what a machine-readable representation of California educator credentials could look like. Nothing here has been published to the Credential Registry, and these CTIDs are not Registry-assigned.

Every credential below is drawn from the Commission on Teacher Credentialing's public Authorization Sort Table, retrieved 2026-08-07, and projected into CTDL as a ceterms:License with its authorized subjects as ceterms:CredentialAlignmentObject alignments. Descriptions, requirements, and renewal terms come from the Commission's own credential leaflets, retrieved 2026-08-07, where a leaflet's published title identifies the authorization and the leaflet page states the code and title the index gave it. The machine-readable output is credentials.jsonld, and the counted coverage statement is coverage.json.

Where a credential below says it has no description, no requirements, or no subject codes, that is the state of the published source and not an omission being smoothed over. Nothing on this page is composed by this project.
