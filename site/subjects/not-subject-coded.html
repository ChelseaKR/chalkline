California credential authorizations published as not subject-coded

Unofficial demonstration. Chalkline is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing or by Credential Engine. It models information the Commission publishes on its public Authorization Sort Table, to show what a machine-readable representation of California educator credentials could look like. Nothing here has been published to the Credential Registry, and these CTIDs are not Registry-assigned.

The Commission publishes NONE in the Subject Code column for each authorization below. That is a published statement about scope, and it is why these are on a page of their own rather than folded in with an empty result.

They are not the same as an authorization whose scope the Commission did not publish in a form this project can read. Those are not modeled at all, and each is listed with its reason in the exclusions table on the main page.
