Unofficial demonstration. Chalkline is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the California Commission on Teacher Credentialing or by Credential Engine. It models information the Commission publishes on its public Authorization Sort Table, to show what a machine-readable representation of California educator credentials could look like. Nothing here has been published to the Credential Registry, and these CTIDs are not Registry-assigned.

Every modeled authorization below carries subject code FLKH in the Commission's published Authorization Sort Table, retrieved 2026-08-07. 5 of them reached the subject through a followed cross-reference rather than through its own rows, and each one says so and names the credential the rows belong to. Nothing on this page is inferred: an authorization appears only where a published row, or a cross-reference this project can follow, puts the code on it. Which assignments a credential permits is a judgement, and this project makes none: it quotes rows.
